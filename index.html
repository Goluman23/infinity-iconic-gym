    Read the full story

    Nitesh Yadav is a fitness educator, entrepreneur and industry leader whose work rests on an uncommon pairing — a Bachelor's in Engineering and a Master's in Exercise Science. Analytical thinking, applied to human performance.

    He began his career in engineering, but the pull of fitness won. Working with reputed gyms, he rose from General Trainer to Fitness Manager, building deep hands-on experience in coaching, management and client transformation — and adding multiple international certifications along the way.

    Education became his calling. At GMSA (Gurumann's Fitness Academy) he mentored aspiring professionals and served as Head of Department for Gujarat and later Gurugram. He went on to found Equilibrium Fitness Academy, where more than 2,000 alumni have trained under his guidance in anatomy, physiology and kinesiology — many now leading successful careers nationwide.

    In 2023 he opened his first fitness club in DLF Phase 3, Gurugram. Its growth led to a second, in Greater Faridabad, in 2025 — the club you're looking at now.
